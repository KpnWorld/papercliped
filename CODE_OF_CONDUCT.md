# Code of conduct

Papercliped is a community project. We want it to be a friendly, welcoming place for everyone, whatever their experience, background or identity.

## What we expect
- Be kind and assume good intent. Disagree with ideas, not people.
- Be patient with newcomers; everyone was new once.
- Give and take feedback gracefully. Credit other people's work.
- Keep discussions on topic and useful for others reading later.

## What is not OK
- Harassment, insults, threats, or discriminatory jokes and language.
- Sexualised content or unwelcome attention.
- Sharing someone's private information without their permission.
- Spam, or repeatedly derailing a discussion after being asked to stop.
- Posting secret keys, tokens or other people's data (even by accident: tell us and we'll help remove it).

## Where it applies
GitHub (issues, discussions, pull requests) and every community space linked from papercliped.co, and when representing the project elsewhere.

## Reporting
Email **support@papercliped.co**. Reports are read by the maintainers and kept confidential. We'll reply, look into it, and may warn, temporarily remove or permanently ban people who break these rules.
